Use The Power Button, Stupid

Published: Wed 26 November 2025
By Tony Williams

In misc.

I needed to reset the password on my M1 Mac Mini, 5 months away from in a hospital you tend to forget passwords.

The keyboard I use has no power key. I tried Command-R, I tried Option and no joy.

Use the power button, stupid. The power button at the rear right works exactly the same as the key top right on aa MacBook keyboard.

Password now changed so I can screen share into it. Now to figure out a fix for mounting the two shares when it reboots.
