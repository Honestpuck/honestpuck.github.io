Character Based Games

Published: Fri 21 November 2025
By Tony Williams

In Recreation.

Today I have been engaged in playing a couple of dungeon crawler games, nethack and Larn.

Both of these are considered "roguelike" after the 1980 game rogue that is the most popular of the early dungeon crawlers. I like nethack as it was the first of them that I played (on a terminal connected to an NCR Tower running System V). I like Larn because it is the only one I've ever managed to complete, once, and at difficulty level 0.

Nethack is fiendishly difficult with a depth of sixty levels and a large range of monsters more than willing to take your life. In 40 years of playing I don't think I've gone past about 40, except in the wizard cheat mode. On a Mac it can be installed with Homebrew.

Larn is a much flatter dungeon with only ten levels. It also has a level 0 with your house and some shops. My problem with Larn is that fall through traps are far too common and I find myself on level 3 before I know it.
