Finally Home From Hospital

Published: Wed 26 November 2025
By Tony Williams

In Personal.

After a little more than 5 months I came home on Monday. To say I am relieved and happy would be an understatement.

There has been a procession of OTs and physios tramping in and out making sur I am set up. They are lending and giving me a bunch of things like a shower stool and a toilet set with grab handles.

I can get around the house quite well on a four wheel walker. I can get up and down the stairs with a cane but I need help getting the walker up and down and handling doors.

This week there has been a cavalcade of people organising various stuff so it seems I will have someone twice a week to make sure I shower safely and more people twice a week to do some household stuff and take me out to shop.
